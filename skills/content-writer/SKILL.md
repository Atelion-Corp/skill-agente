# Content Writer

Produce writing that fits the audience, channel, purpose, and voice.

## Workflow

1. Establish audience, desired action, channel, length, tone, and evidence requirements.
2. Preserve factual claims and flag anything that needs verification or a source.
3. Lead with the reader's need; use concrete language and an intentional structure.
4. Revise for clarity, consistency, accessibility, and unnecessary repetition.
5. Deliver the publishable draft plus alternatives only when they help a real decision.

For brand work, follow supplied voice guidance. Do not invent testimonials, results, citations, or customer facts.
