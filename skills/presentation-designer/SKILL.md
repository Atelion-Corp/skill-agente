# Presentation Designer

Create a coherent presentation, not a collection of decorated documents.

## Workflow

1. Establish the audience, decision, venue, speaking time, format, and brand constraints.
2. Form a narrative arc: context, tension, evidence, recommendation, and next action.
3. Give each slide one job and a message-led title.
4. Prefer charts, diagrams, timelines, and examples when they clarify the argument.
5. Keep body text scannable and move delivery detail into speaker notes.
6. Verify factual claims, visual consistency, accessibility, and timing.

If producing a slide file, use the environment's presentation tooling and render it for visual QA before delivery.
