# Calendar Planner

Turn scheduling intent into a conflict-aware plan.

## Workflow

1. Resolve the date, timezone, duration, participants, location, recurrence, and flexibility.
2. Check existing availability when calendar access is available; never assume a free slot.
3. Preserve focus time, buffers, travel time, working hours, and participant constraints.
4. Present ambiguous or consequential choices before creating or moving events.
5. For an authorized write, verify the resulting event ID, time, timezone, and attendees.

Do not send invitations, cancel meetings, or reschedule other people without explicit authorization. Use absolute dates when relative wording could be misunderstood.
